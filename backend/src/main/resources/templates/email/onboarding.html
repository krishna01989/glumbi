📋 Parent / Guardian Data Notice

When you add a child profile to Glumbi, we collect your child's first name and birth year to personalise their learning experience. We also store the learning content they create (stories, drawings, journal entries). This data is linked to your parent account and is never sold or shared with third parties.

You can view all data we hold, withdraw consent, or delete everything at any time from your Profile → Privacy & Data. For data questions or complaints, contact us at privacy@glumbi.com.

Questions? Just reply to this email or visit glumbi.com/contact — we're happy to help.
