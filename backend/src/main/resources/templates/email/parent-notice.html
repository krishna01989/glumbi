A quick note about your child's data

We've updated our privacy practices to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and COPPA (USA). As a parent or guardian using Glumbi, we want to be clear about what we collect, why, and the controls available to you.

What we collect about your child

📛  First name and birth year — to personalise stories and activities
📖  Stories, drawings, journal entries, and curiosity answers your child creates
📊  Feature usage timestamps — shown to you as a weekly activity summary

What we never do

🚫  We never sell your data or share it with advertisers
🚫  We never profile or behaviourally target children
🚫  We never use tracking cookies or third-party analytics scripts

Your rights as a parent

✅  View all data — Profile → Privacy & Data → My Data
✅  Withdraw consent — Profile → Privacy & Data → Withdraw Parental Consent
✅  Delete everything — Profile → Delete Account (or email us)

📋 We need your consent to continue

Next time you log in to Glumbi, you'll see a short consent prompt before accessing the app. This records your agreement as a parent or guardian that we may process your child's data as described above. It takes one tap and is required by law.
